Bat and ball

From Thinking, Fast and Slow, chapter 3 – an answer that is intuitive, appealing, and wrong.

Do not try to solve the puzzle – listen to your intuition. You have seconds to type the first answer that comes to mind.

A bat and ball cost $1.10.

The bat costs one dollar more than the ball.

How much does the ball cost?

A number came to your mind. The number, of course, is 10: 10¢. The distinctive mark of this easy puzzle is that it evokes an answer that is intuitive, appealing, and wrong.

Do the math: if the ball costs 10¢, the bat costs $1.10 and the total is $1.20, not $1.10. The correct answer is 5¢ – the bat then costs $1.05, one dollar more, and together they cost $1.10.

The quick answer comes from System 1. Checking it takes only a moment of System 2, yet most people – including many students at top universities – do not make that small effort.
