Thinking, Fast and Slow

Small exercises to try out ideas from Daniel Kahneman's book.

  • Müller-Lyer illusion Chapter 1 – which line is longer? An impression System 1 will not give up.
  • Conflict task Chapter 1 – judge position or letter case while the word itself says the opposite.
  • Add-1 task Chapter 2 – increment digits to a metronome and feel System 2 at full effort.
