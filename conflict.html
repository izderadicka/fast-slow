Conflict task

From Thinking, Fast and Slow, chapter 1 – when an automatic reaction gets in the way of the task, System 2 has to overrule System 1.

Two short rounds. In each you answer a simple question about how a word is printed, while ignoring what the word says. Answer as fast as you can.

RoundWord agreesWord conflictsSlower byErrors

Times are averages over correct answers. You read the word automatically even though you were told to ignore it; when its meaning disagrees with the right answer, System 2 must suppress that reading, which costs time and causes mistakes.
