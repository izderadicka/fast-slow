Add-1 task

From Thinking, Fast and Slow, chapter 2 – a feel for System 2 working at full effort.

  1. A metronome beats once per second. Read the digits aloud, one per beat.
  2. The card disappears. Wait for two beats.
  3. Say the new string, one digit per beat, each digit increased by the increment (9 wraps to 0, so with Add-1 5294 becomes 6305).
  4. Type the digits as you say them and the page checks the answer for you. You get two extra beats to finish typing.
  5. The next card follows right away, until you press Stop.

Tip: if holding the original digits is too much, read the card aloud already incremented (see 5294, say “6305”) and just repeat that string when reporting. It is easier, because the adding is done while the card is still visible.
