Müller-Lyer illusion

From Thinking, Fast and Slow, chapter 1 – an impression System 1 will not give up.

Look at the two horizontal lines. Which one is longer?

The rulers show that both lines have exactly the same length. Now hide the rulers: you know the lines are equal, yet the bottom one still looks longer. System 2 has the belief, but it cannot stop System 1 from producing the illusion.
